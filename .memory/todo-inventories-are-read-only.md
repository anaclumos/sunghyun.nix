---
name: todo-inventories-are-read-only
description: Shut-down TODO inventories from retired repos are handoff docs, not standing mandates
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

A shut-down TODO inventory left by a prior session is a read-only handoff document. A pointer to it ("finish TODO.md") does not authorize resuming its items or editing it; the explicitly requested work (owner, 2026-08-08: package adds only) is the whole scope.

**Why:** Resuming inventoried items without a fresh mandate re-opens work the owner deliberately stopped.

**How to apply:** Treat such files as context. Act only on the owner's explicit ask in the current session; do not edit the inventory itself.
